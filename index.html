06 / What is it learning?
A quick guide to the experiment
FIELD NOTES

State: normalized position, relative goal direction, and four nearby wall sensors.

Reward: reaching the goal is good, wasted steps and wall collisions are costly, and moving closer earns a small shaping reward.

Learning: the agent stores experiences, samples a random batch, and updates its Q-network with a temporal-difference target.

Important: learning is not guaranteed to be smooth. Neural networks can get stuck, forget useful behavior, or need more episodes. Try a new seed and compare.
